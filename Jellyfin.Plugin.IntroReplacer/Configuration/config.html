Intro Replacer

Plays a custom intro before the episode or movie you start, then lets you skip the item's own intro.

Settings

Turning this on skips episodes and seasons. Some TV and mobile apps do not ask for intros on episodes anyway, while movies work everywhere.
Used by the "Only after the wait time" frequency below.

Intro files

Upload a video file, or pick any video that is already in one of your libraries. Nothing is copied and your media is never modified.

Rules

Rules are evaluated from top to bottom and the first enabled rule that matches wins. A rule with no condition matches every item. Replace lets the client skip the item's own intro, Prepend plays the item from the start.
